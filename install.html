Add Dry? to your home screen. It opens like an app and finds your state from your location the moment you tap it.

  1. Open this site in Safari. Chrome on iPhone works too on iOS 16.4 and later.
  2. Tap the Share button at the bottom of the screen.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Keep the name Dry? and tap Add.
  5. Open it from your home screen and tap Allow when it asks for your location.
  1. Open this site in Chrome.
  2. Tap the ⋮ menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen or Install app.
  4. Confirm with Install.
  5. Open it and allow location. It finds your state every time you launch it.
