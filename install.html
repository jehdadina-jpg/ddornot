Know before you go.

Add Dry? to your home screen. Every time you open it, it checks the state you're in.

  1. Open this page in Safari.
  2. Tap Share in the toolbar.
  3. Scroll down and tap Add to Home Screen.
  4. Tap Add, then open Dry? from your home screen.
  5. Tap Allow when it asks for your location.
  1. Open this page in Chrome.
  2. Tap menu at the top right.
  3. Tap Add to Home screen, then Install.
  4. Open Dry? and allow location.
  1. Open the Shortcuts app and tap +.
  2. Add the Open URLs action and paste
  3. Name the shortcut Is it a dry day.
  4. Now say "Hey Siri, is it a dry day".

Your location stays on your phone. It's only used to work out your state, and you can always pick one by hand.
